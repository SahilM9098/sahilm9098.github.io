Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be processed when you use the ARK Mobile Catalog website.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Information you provide

The catalog does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal information in order to search the catalog or copy commands.

Basic technical data

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most web hosting services, the hosting platform may process technical request information needed to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser/device information, requested pages, and timestamps. The site owner does not use the catalog itself to ask for or store this information.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This website may use Google AdSense to display advertisements when advertising is enabled. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, or similar technologies to provide, measure, limit, and personalize advertising depending on your consent choices and applicable law.

You can learn more about how Google uses information in advertising at Google's advertising technologies page and review Google's broader Privacy Policy.

Consent for European users

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the site may use Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). When shown, the consent message lets you consent, decline consent, or manage your choices. Advertising behavior may change based on those choices.

Cookies

The catalog functionality itself does not need advertising cookies to perform searches or generate commands. Third-party services such as Google AdSense may set or access cookies or similar storage when their services are enabled and permitted.

Third-party links

The website may link to third-party websites such as GitHub or Google. Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies, which are outside the control of this site.

Children's privacy

This website is a general-purpose game reference and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should follow the age requirements and parental controls that apply to the services and games they use.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when site features, advertising, hosting, or privacy requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or site corrections, use the public GitHub project at github.com/SahilM9098/ark-mobile-catalog.

This privacy policy describes the current simple static-site setup. If analytics, accounts, forms, or additional third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them.
